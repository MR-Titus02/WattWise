IS 1106 · Semester 1 group project

Energy awareness starts with understanding.

WattWise is a community-focused educational website that makes household electricity use easier to explore—one appliance and one everyday choice at a time.

⌂

Our purpose

Make energy information approachable for everyone.

Why WattWise?

Many people use a range of electrical appliances without an easy way to connect a wattage label and daily hours of use to an energy estimate. WattWise explains that relationship in simple terms and offers a place to explore everyday appliance examples and household habits.

It is designed with Sri Lankan households in mind: costs are shown in LKR, examples feature familiar appliances, and no official electricity rate is assumed.

Learning that can be shared

A useful starting point for the community.

Understanding usage can support more informed conversations and choices at home. WattWise helps by:

  • 01

    Making calculations easier to follow

    Clear inputs and a simple formula explain how wattage and hours become kWh.

  • 02

    Putting common appliances in context

    Approximate examples help people get started, while encouraging them to check their own labels.

  • 03

    Sharing practical ideas

    Household tips invite people to consider everyday usage without promising a particular bill reduction.

Project limitations

WattWise is educational, not an official electricity billing platform. It does not read meters, predict bills, or transmit personal data. Estimates depend on user inputs, appliance behavior, and the user's chosen rate.

WattWise is a student project for IS 1106 – Foundations of Web Technologies. Appliance ratings are approximate examples, tips are general ideas, and actual savings or bills may differ.

Describe the static site, expected pages, core interactions, and explicit exclusions. Note that the project has no backend, database, or authentication.

## Target users and assumptions

Identify intended users, accessibility considerations, and assumptions that the team must test or document.

## Deliverables and schedule

Add agreed deliverables, milestones, owners, and dates after the team confirms them.
